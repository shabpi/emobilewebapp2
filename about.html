WHO WE ARE

An engineering company. Not a reseller.

EMobility Egypt is a specialized engineering and technology company delivering integrated solutions across EV charging infrastructure, industrial automation, smart electrical systems, SCADA & RTU integration, critical power, and digital infrastructure.

We operate through a complete project lifecycle — from engineering and design through system integration, supply and manufacturing, installation and commissioning, FAT/SAT testing, technical support, operation and maintenance, and training services. Every project carries one engineering team and one point of accountability.
